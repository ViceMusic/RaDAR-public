ABOUT THE DATASET

关于 RaDAR-Bench

513 SIF measurements402 SGF measurements

RaDAR-Bench is a KAUST-curated, multi-source benchmark for peptide stability prediction in simulated intestinal fluid (SIF) and simulated gastric fluid (SGF). It comprises 513 molecular records from five patent-derived sources, including 513 SIF and 402 SGF measurements. With standardized molecular structures, harmonized stability annotations, and preserved source provenance, RaDAR-Bench supports molecular property prediction, chemical-space analysis, reproducible benchmarking, and cross-source generalization research.

For questions about the dataset, its processing, or benchmark settings, feel free to contact Tiange (Hokkaido University) at tian@lmd.ist.hokudai.ac.jp.

可视化与数据说明

基于指定的四个训练／测试 CSV 整理，保留重复记录。浏览序号只表示文件内顺序，不使用原始 ID。Morgan 指纹：radius 2、1024 bits、包含手性。预计算一个三维 UMAP（Jaccard 距离、15 个近邻、min_dist 0.12、随机种子 42），二维为其正交 XY 投影，不是单独拟合的二维 UMAP。

侧栏保留原始稳定性标记；星号及等级的分钟数属于项目映射值。sif_sgf_second 为数据文件保留的来源名称。侧栏只展示数据记录，不进行预测。
